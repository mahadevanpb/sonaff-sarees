Our Story

Curating India's finest sarees, one loom at a time

Who We Are

Not a store.
A curation.

SonAff Saree was born from a simple belief: every woman deserves access to the most authentic, handcrafted sarees that India's master weavers create without having to travel thousands of kilometres to find them.

We are a Bengaluru-based team of saree enthusiasts who travel across India's greatest weaving heartlands Varanasi, Kanchipuram, Chanderi, Pochampally, Patan, Bhagalpur to discover exclusive collections directly from the hands that make them. Every piece we carry has been personally selected for its craftsmanship, authenticity, and beauty.

Starting with customers across Karnataka, our mission is to eventually bring these living treasures to saree lovers all over India.

How We Work

Our Quality Promise

Authenticity Guaranteed

Every saree we carry is genuine sourced directly from weavers and verified for authenticity. No power-loom imitations, no shortcuts.

Handpicked, Not Bulk-Bought

We don't stock warehouses. Each piece is individually selected by us in person, which means every saree you see is one we genuinely love.

Direct Weaver Support

By sourcing directly from artisan clusters, we ensure weavers receive fair prices for their craft and their traditions continue to thrive.

Personal Service

Have questions before you buy? Message us on WhatsApp. We know every saree in our collection and will help you find the perfect one.
