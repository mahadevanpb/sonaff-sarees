Pattern Usage Guidelines

✓ Use at ≤20% opacity as background texture
✓ Apply as border / divider accent strips
✓ Use in gold on dark charcoal backgrounds
✗ Never use full-opacity pattern as primary background
✗ Never place text directly over a dense pattern
✗ Never mix more than two motif styles in one layout
05 Voice & Tone

The Brand Voice

"We believe authenticity shines brightest in daily life
our sarees are crafted to be worn, loved, and lived in with joy."
Sonaff Sarees Brand Manifesto
We Are
  • Warm & welcoming
  • Knowledgeable but not elitist
  • Proud of heritage without being preachy
  • Personal we speak woman to woman
  • Poetic when describing craft
We Are Not
  • Corporate or stiff
  • Overly promotional or pushy
  • Condescending about tradition
  • Excessive with superlatives
  • Trendy we are timeless
Language Style
  • Use em-dashes for rhythm not hyphens
  • Avoid exclamation marks in body copy
  • Prefer "crafted" over "made" or "manufactured"
  • Use "heritage" not "old" or "traditional"
  • Always name the region of origin

Copy Examples

✓ Do Write This

"Each Banarasi Silk saree is handwoven by master craftspeople in Varanasi a city where the loom has spoken for over 1,000 years."

✗ Avoid This

"SALE!! Amazing Banarasi sarees at the BEST PRICES!!! Buy now before they're gone!"

07 Do & Don't

Usage Rules

These rules protect the integrity of the Sonaff brand across all touchpoints digital, print, and social.

✓ Do

Use Approved Backgrounds

Always place the logo on: deep charcoal, warm cream, or white backgrounds. These ensure maximum legibility and brand consistency.

Sonaff Sarees
Sonaff Sarees
✗ Don't

Don't Place on Busy Backgrounds

Never place the logo directly over a photograph, pattern, or colourful gradient without a clear semi-transparent overlay first.

Sonaff Sarees
✓ Do

Pair Fonts Correctly

Use Playfair Display for headlines and Inter for body copy. The contrast between serif and sans-serif creates elegant hierarchy.

Heritage Handwovens

Crafted by master weavers across India's celebrated loom traditions.

✗ Don't

Don't Use Unapproved Fonts

Never use Comic Sans, Arial, Times New Roman, or any system font as a display font. These undermine the premium brand positioning.

Heritage Handwovens

Crafted by weavers in India.

✓ Do

Use Gold Sparingly

Gold is a premium accent. Use it for CTAs, key highlights, and decorative elements never as a primary background colour.

✗ Don't

Don't Overuse Gold

Filling entire pages, backgrounds, or illustrations with gold #D78C2F makes the brand look cheap. Gold only works when it's a highlight.
